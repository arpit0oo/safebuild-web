— SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Engineered for Continuous Operation

The 10-Ton Double Girder EOT Crane is designed to provide robust lifting capabilities in demanding manufacturing environments. Featuring dual drive technology and a high-strength structural frame, it ensures stability and precision during critical material handling tasks.

Equipped with advanced end carriage assemblies, the system offers smooth acceleration and deceleration, minimizing load sway. The M5 duty cycle rating guarantees reliability under continuous operational stress, making it the ideal choice for heavy assembly lines and steel fabrication workshops.
